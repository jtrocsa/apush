Unit 8

Period 8: 1945–1980

Cold War, civil rights, postwar prosperity, social change, Vietnam, liberalism, conservatism, and distrust of government.

Exam weighting: 10–17% 15 CED topics

Unit Overview

Cold War, civil rights, postwar prosperity, social change, Vietnam, liberalism, conservatism, and distrust of government.

Use this page as your landing place for notes, review, and historical thinking practice. The outline below is paraphrased from the AP U.S. History CED topic structure.

Practice Focus

For this unit, prioritize these AP habits:

  • Explain developments in context.
  • Use evidence to support a claim.
  • Connect causes, effects, and continuities.

Unit Objectives

  • Explain how the Cold War shaped foreign policy and domestic life.
  • Analyze postwar economic growth and its uneven effects.
  • Evaluate civil rights movements’ strategies, achievements, and limits.
  • Explain the causes and consequences of the Vietnam War and 1970s distrust.
  • Practice continuity and change across postwar liberalism, activism, and conservatism.
