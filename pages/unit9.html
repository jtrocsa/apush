Unit 9

Period 9: 1980–Present

Conservatism, globalization, changing demographics, technology, terrorism, polarization, and contemporary America.

Exam weighting: 4–6% 7 CED topics

Unit Overview

Conservatism, globalization, changing demographics, technology, terrorism, polarization, and contemporary America.

Use this page as your landing place for notes, review, and historical thinking practice. The outline below is paraphrased from the AP U.S. History CED topic structure.

Practice Focus

For this unit, prioritize these AP habits:

  • Explain developments in context.
  • Use evidence to support a claim.
  • Connect causes, effects, and continuities.

Unit Objectives

  • Explain the rise and influence of modern conservatism.
  • Analyze globalization, technology, and demographic change.
  • Explain the end of the Cold War and the emergence of new global challenges.
  • Evaluate political polarization and debates over government’s role.
  • Practice causation with contemporary historical developments.

CED-Based Topic Outline

Topic Focus What students should be able to do
9.1 Contextualizing Period 9 Explain the context for political, economic, and social change since 1980.
9.2 Reagan and Conservatism Analyze the rise of modern conservatism and Reagan-era policies.
9.3 The End of the Cold War Explain the causes and effects of the Cold War’s end.
9.4 A Changing Economy Analyze globalization, deindustrialization, technology, and economic inequality.
9.5 Migration and Immigration in the 1990s and 2000s Explain demographic change and debates over immigration.
9.6 Challenges of the 21st Century Analyze terrorism, wars, technology, climate, and political polarization.
9.7 Causation in Period 9 Evaluate causes and effects of major developments from 1980 to the present.
