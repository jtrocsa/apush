Unit 2

Period 2: 1607–1754

Colonial settlement, regional development, labor systems, imperial competition, and the Atlantic World.

Exam weighting: 6–8% 8 CED topics

Unit Overview

Colonial settlement, regional development, labor systems, imperial competition, and the Atlantic World.

Use this page as your landing place for notes, review, and historical thinking practice. The outline below is paraphrased from the AP U.S. History CED topic structure.

Practice Focus

For this unit, prioritize these AP habits:

  • Explain developments in context.
  • Use evidence to support a claim.
  • Connect causes, effects, and continuities.

Unit Objectives

  • Compare European models of colonization.
  • Explain why British colonial regions developed differently.
  • Analyze the growth of slavery and racial hierarchy in the colonies.
  • Explain how trade and empire connected North America to the Atlantic World.
  • Practice comparison using colonial regions and imperial systems.

CED-Based Topic Outline

Topic Focus What students should be able to do
2.1 Contextualizing Period 2 Explain major developments in European colonization between 1607 and 1754.
2.2 European Colonization Compare Spanish, French, Dutch, and British approaches to colonization.
2.3 The Regions of British Colonies Explain how regional differences emerged in British North America.
2.4 Transatlantic Trade Analyze how commerce connected the colonies, Europe, Africa, and the Caribbean.
2.5 Interactions Between American Indians and Europeans Explain patterns of cooperation, conflict, negotiation, and resistance.
2.6 Slavery in the British Colonies Explain the development and consequences of slavery in colonial North America.
2.7 Colonial Society and Culture Analyze religious, political, social, and intellectual developments in colonial society.
2.8 Comparison in Period 2 Compare colonial regions, imperial systems, and labor arrangements.

CED Timeline

1607Jamestown founded

England’s first permanent colony in North America marked the beginning of a new colonial era.

1619House of Burgesses and enslaved labor expand

Virginia’s representative government and the growth of slavery helped define colonial society.

1620Mayflower Compact

Puritan settlers created an early example of self-government in New England.

1676Bacon’s Rebellion

Class tensions and frontier conflict pushed colonists to rely more heavily on racial slavery.

1754French and Indian War begins

Imperial conflict in North America intensified rivalries that would shape later revolutionary politics.
