Unit 7

Period 7: 1890–1945

Progressivism, imperialism, World War I, the 1920s, the Great Depression, the New Deal, and World War II.

Exam weighting: 10–17% 15 CED topics

Unit Overview

Progressivism, imperialism, World War I, the 1920s, the Great Depression, the New Deal, and World War II.

Use this page as your landing place for notes, review, and historical thinking practice. The outline below is paraphrased from the AP U.S. History CED topic structure.

Practice Focus

For this unit, prioritize these AP habits:

  • Explain developments in context.
  • Use evidence to support a claim.
  • Connect causes, effects, and continuities.

Unit Objectives

  • Analyze the causes and effects of Progressive reform.
  • Explain debates over U.S. expansion and global power.
  • Evaluate how World War I and World War II changed American society and government.
  • Explain the causes and effects of the Great Depression and New Deal.
  • Practice comparison across reform eras, wars, and crises.

CED-Based Topic Outline

Topic Focus What students should be able to do
7.1 Contextualizing Period 7 Explain the context for reform, global conflict, and modern America.
7.2 Imperialism: Debates Analyze arguments for and against overseas expansion.
7.3 The Spanish–American War Explain causes and effects of the war and new overseas possessions.
7.4 The Progressives Explain goals, strategies, and limits of Progressive reform.
7.5 World War I: Military and Diplomacy Analyze U.S. entry, wartime diplomacy, and the war’s consequences.
7.6 World War I: Home Front Explain how war affected civil liberties, labor, migration, and society.
7.7 1920s: Innovations in Communication and Technology Analyze cultural and economic changes of the 1920s.
7.8 1920s: Cultural and Political Controversies Explain conflicts over race, immigration, religion, gender, and modernity.
7.9 The Great Depression Explain causes and effects of the Great Depression.
7.10 The New Deal Analyze New Deal goals, programs, critics, and long-term effects.
7.11 Interwar Foreign Policy Explain U.S. foreign policy between the world wars.
7.12 World War II: Mobilization Analyze the home front and wartime mobilization.
7.13 World War II: Military Explain major military developments and consequences.
7.14 Postwar Diplomacy Analyze wartime diplomacy and the origins of the Cold War.
7.15 Comparison in Period 7 Compare reform movements, wars, and government responses from 1890 to 1945.
